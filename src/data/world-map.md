# World map

`world-map.json` contains simplified country outlines projected with the Natural
Earth projection. Source: Natural Earth, public-domain geographic data, distributed
by [datasets/geo-countries](https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries).

Source file: `data/countries.geojson`, retrieved for this implementation. Outlines
were simplified and small islands omitted for readability at dashboard size.
Antarctica is omitted. Borders are illustrative and do not imply a position on
territorial status.

The risk scores in `geopolitical.ts` are **demonstration data**, not measurements
from Natural Earth, the official GPR index, or any risk-rating provider.
